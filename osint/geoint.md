# 🌍 GEOINT Cheat Sheet

## 📌 Methodology
1. **Metadata Extraction:** Check EXIF data for GPS coordinates and camera details.
2. **Visual Clues:** Analyze landmarks, architecture, license plates, signage, shadows, and vegetation.
3. **Verification:** Cross-reference locations using satellite imagery, street views, and sun-angle tools.

---

## 🛠️ CLI Tools & Commands
| Tool | Description | Quick Command |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **ExifTool** | Read & edit EXIF image metadata | `exiftool image.jpg` |
| **Geoguessr Tools** | Tools for GEOINT automation | `python3 tool.py -i image.jpg` |

---

## 🌐 Reverse Image Search
* **Google Lens:** `lens.google.com` (Best for general objects & places)
* **Yandex Images:** `yandex.com/images` (Best for landscapes & landmark detection)
* **PimEyes:** `pimeyes.com` (Face recognition and search)
* **TinEye:** `tineye.com` (Exact image duplicates & history)

---

## 🗺️ Mapping & Satellite Services
* **Google Earth Pro:** Historical satellite imagery & 3D buildings
* **SunCalc:** `suncalc.org` (Shadow angle & time-of-day estimation)
* **PeakFinder:** `peakfinder.org` (Mountain ranges horizon identification)
* **Overpass Turbo:** `overpass-turbo.eu` (OpenStreetMap query builder)

---

## 🔍 Key Visual Indicators
* **Traffic & License Plates:** Driving side (Left/Right), plate design & colors.
* **Infrastructure:** Power outlets/plugs, pole types, road markings, taxi colors.
* **Nature:** Plant/tree species, soil color, sun/shadow direction.
